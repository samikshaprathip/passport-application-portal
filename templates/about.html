About Us

Our Vision

To empower every Indian citizen with hassle-free access to international travel through a modern, secure, and efficient passport issuance system.

Mission Statement

  • Streamline the passport issuance process through digitization
  • Ensure accessibility through widespread Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs)
  • Maintain high standards of security and verification
  • Provide responsive support for all passport-related needs

Nationwide Coverage

Over 500+ Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs) and Post Office PSKs (POPSKs) are operational across the country to ensure every citizen has access to services.
